## Pattern worth keeping

Three of the seven HIGH/CRITICAL findings were **regressions introduced by the morning's own fixes** (B-111 → R-B-01, B-102 → R-B-02, `supersedes` → R-O-02). A fix that passes its own unit test is not evidence the system works; the only tests that caught these were the ones that boot the real router, send what the real client sends, and assert on the side effect. Keep writing that kind of test for every middleware or protocol change, and keep running an independent review on any day with more than a few thousand lines of change.
